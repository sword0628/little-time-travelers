02 / Growing into next season

Can new gear last two winters?

Possibly—but buying now does not guarantee a fit at age 6½.

  • Board: compare her weight and ability with the exact model’s size chart now and next year. Her 117 cm height alone cannot select a board.
  • Bindings: can remain useful if next year’s boots still fit their published range and adjustment limits.
  • Boots: the largest uncertainty. Do not oversize for future growth. No growth-expansion feature was verified for the Nitro Droid listing.
  • Helmet: may last if it remains undamaged and correctly fits within its adjustment range. Recheck fit each season.

Needed for a fitted shortlist: weight, foot length / shoe size, head circumference, and riding experience.
