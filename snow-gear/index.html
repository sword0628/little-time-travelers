Why this board: the Jones size chart puts 100 cm at 45–65 lb, matching her 50 lb weight. The 110 cm size starts at 55 lb. Jones describes the Prodigy as suitable for learning and rates its flex 4/10. A 100 cm board is a model-specific recommendation, not a general 100–115 cm rule.

Planning for two seasons: keep the 100 cm recommendation for her current beginner stage. It could work next year if she remains within the 45–65 lb range and the stance, boot width, and riding needs still fit. The Jones 110 cm starts at 55 lb; do not move up solely to extend its lifespan. Recheck fit before next season rather than assuming age or height guarantees another year.

Other options reviewed: Burton After School Special 100 cm is rated 40–55 lb and includes bindings. Salomon Team Package 100 cm is a soft beginner board/binding package. Jones provides a documented weight match, more stated weight headroom than the Burton 100, and a board-only purchase that preserves the separate binding comparison. Neither next-year fit nor compatibility with an unmeasured boot is guaranteed.

Practical option: rent board/bindings/boots for $220 and buy the $88 Smith helmet: $308 plus helmet tax. Her boots and bindings need a foot-size check before purchase.
