Binding guide · softness, sizing and reversible ankle straps

Soft bindings have more give for a gentler response while boots remain securely held. Medium flex balances support and movement; stiff bindings transfer inputs more directly. For your beginner setup, favor soft-to-medium flex. Medium boot size and medium flex are separate specifications; numerical flex scales vary between brands.

The RIDE A-8 reversible ankle strap can be swapped between bindings: stiff section toward the top for a more aggressive feel, soft section toward the top for more flexibility and lateral movement. Follow RIDE’s installation instructions. This changes strap feel, not highback or chassis stiffness. Burton’s soft-versus-stiff binding guide · Niche Wraith family specifications. Current Niche specifications do not establish the used 2021/22 board’s exact specifications.
